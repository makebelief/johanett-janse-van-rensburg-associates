WELCOME TO THE FIRM

Johanett Janse van Rensburg & Associates Inc..

A personal approach, with attention to every detail.

Based in central Gqeberha, the firm offers clients practical assistance across property transactions, deceased estate matters, family law, civil litigation, contracts and related disputes.

Founded in 2024, the practice is directed by attorney and conveyancer Johanett Janse van Rensburg, whose background includes litigation, conveyancing, debt collections and deceased estates.
